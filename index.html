OpenOracle

Tags are fingers. The Oracle keeps Dawn’s public face true, everywhere.

Status

Founding — initialized, first release in preparation

What it is

The canonical source and operator for every Dawn product’s public-facing metadata, across every platform and language. One canon entry per product per surface — YouTube tags and descriptions, channel keywords, app-store listings, social bios, SEO meta, link-media-card text — edited once in canon, propagated everywhere. Tags are fingers: the tag is the finger pointing at the moon, the content is the moon. SEO’s essence is standing on the road where people search — that is OpenOracle’s work, stated plainly.

END GOAL

All of Dawn’s canonical data served through the Oracle for all AIs and all people to use. Anywhere an AI or a form needs a value — a tag, a date, a price, a fact — the Oracle supplies the canonical one, with its label and source attached. Hallucination is what happens in the Oracle’s absence. Stale public text is treated as a defect; the founder never pastes a keyword again.

ROADMAP

  • One Oracle, three faces: DawnSci fills numbers, OpenVehicle fills facts, OpenOracle fills the public face — one canonical data service beneath all three, callable by any AI via API/MCP and by any human via the school.
  • The fill-in principle: canonical values supplied with label and source attached, everywhere a value is needed.
  • Free tier with attribution — every answer carries Dawn as its source; paid volume/commercial tiers for AIs and companies that fill at scale.
  • Every fill is logged; every correction improves the canon for every future fill.

Roadmap drawn from the founding design seed (2026-11-21).
